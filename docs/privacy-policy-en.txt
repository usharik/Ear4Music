Privacy Policy for Ear4Music

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Ear4Music is an ear-training app for Android and iOS. Its learning features work locally on your device. This policy describes both versions; where they differ, it says so.

1. Personal data
The app does not require an account and does not ask for personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or microphone recordings.

2. Internet access
Android: the app uses the Internet to load advertising, to communicate with Firebase services (only if you agreed, see section 4) and with Google Play for the optional purchase.
iOS: the app uses the Internet only to communicate with Firebase services, and only if you agreed (see section 4). Otherwise it works fully offline and sends no data to the developer or to third parties.

3. Advertising (Android)
The Android app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads on the task list and training screens and, occasionally, a full-screen (interstitial) ad before a training session or when you choose to open an exercise ahead of your progress. When ads are requested or shown, Google and its partners may process technical and advertising-related data according to their own privacy policies, for example device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and ad interaction data. See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the AdMob documentation.
The iOS app currently shows no ads. If advertising is added to it, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it (including Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt).

4. Firebase services (Android and iOS) — only with your consent
On first launch the app asks whether you want to help improve it by sharing usage statistics and crash reports. Nothing is collected until you agree, and you can change your answer at any time with the "Usage data" switch at the bottom of the task list. If you agree, both apps use the following Firebase services provided by Google:
- Firebase Analytics: usage data such as screen names, exercise events and results (score, correct, wrong and missed counts) and session information, to understand how the app is used.
- Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and device diagnostics (device model, OS and app version), to find and fix technical problems.
- Firebase Performance Monitoring: performance data such as app start-up time and the duration of a training session.
Each installation is identified by a random Firebase installation identifier (the iOS app does not read the identifier for vendor, IDFV), and Google derives an approximate location (country or city) from the IP address of each request for regional statistics. Google keeps Analytics data for at most 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites; the iOS app does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) and therefore shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt. The data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

5. Purchases (Android)
The Android app offers an optional one-time purchase that removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app only receives confirmation of whether the purchase is owned and never sees your payment details.

6. Reminders
If you agree, the app shows a daily reminder to practise (around 11:00). Reminders are created and shown on your device: AlarmManager notifications on Android, local notifications on iOS. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them. You can turn them off at any time in the system notification settings.

7. Data stored on your device
The app stores training data locally on your device: exercise progress and session results (notes played, correct, wrong and missed answers), the selected language and the reminder settings. This data is not transmitted to the developer or to any server, except that, if you agreed to share usage data (section 4), the results of each session are included in the usage statistics.

8. Backups
Android: system backup is enabled for the app; depending on your device and Google backup settings, the locally stored app data may be included in your backups.
iOS: the app's data may be included in your iCloud or computer backups of the device, according to your device settings.

9. Data deletion
You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app, or on Android in Settings → Apps → Ear4Music → Storage → Clear data. Data already sent to Firebase is deleted automatically after the retention periods in section 4; turning usage data off stops any further sending. To have it deleted earlier, contact us (section 12).

10. Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. The advertising in the Android app is provided by Google under its own policies.

11. Changes to this policy
This policy is updated when the app's functionality changes; the date above shows the latest version.

12. Contact
Questions about this policy: see the app's page at https://usharik.dev/apps/ear4music/
